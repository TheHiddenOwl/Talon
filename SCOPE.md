# Legal Scope & Policy

Talon is designed for authorized OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) gathering.

## Responsibility
Users of Talon are responsible for ensuring that their use of the tool complies with all applicable laws, regulations, and terms of service of the data sources accessed.

## Authorization
Before using Talon to scan any domain or infrastructure, you must ensure you have explicit authorization or that the collection activities fall within the legal bounds of your jurisdiction and the policies of the targets and data providers.

## --confirm-scope
Talon enforces a `--confirm-scope` flag for all commands that interact with external services. This serves as a mandatory acknowledgement of the user's responsibility and authorization.

## Data Sources
Talon interacts with various public APIs and services (e.g., crt.sh, Wayback Machine, Shodan). Users must respect the Terms of Service of these individual providers.
